E-GROUND
Tap the ground to move the placement spot · drag a cracker card up onto the ground to place it
\1

Everything is done directly in the world. Crackers opens the catalogue: drag a cracker onto the ground (or tap it, then tap the ground). Drag a placed cracker to move it; right-drag or scroll over it (two-finger twist on touch) to rotate; hold Alt/Ctrl while dragging, or long-press on touch, to copy; drag it into the copper Return bin near the entrance to remove it. Drag a Phuljhadi onto the hand area to hold it, click or tap its glowing tip to light it, then touch a fuse and hold to ignite. Move: WASD or joystick; look: drag (right-drag or two fingers while holding a sparkler); wheel or pinch to zoom.

All effects are digital simulations only.
