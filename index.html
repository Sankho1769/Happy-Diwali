E-GROUND
Tap the ground to move the placement spot · drag a cracker card up onto the ground to place it
Controls

Desktop: WASD move, Shift faster, drag mouse to look, wheel to zoom, click ground to place, Space = ignite, H = hold, C = catalogue.
Mobile: left joystick to move, drag to look, pinch to zoom, tap ground to place, drag a card up to drop it.
Ignite: Light it (tap) or press and hold Hold to ignite to watch the fuse burn; release early to cancel.

All effects are digital simulations only.
